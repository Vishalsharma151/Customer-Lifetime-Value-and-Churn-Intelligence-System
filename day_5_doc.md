# Notebook 07 — Customer Retention Strategy and Prioritization

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Customer Lifetime Value and Churn Intelligence System  
**Notebook:** 07 — Customer Retention Strategy and Prioritization  
**Dataset:** IBM Telco Customer Churn  
**Technology Stack:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, LightGBM, SHAP, Lifelines

## Objective

The objective of this notebook is to identify customers who may be at risk of leaving the telecom service and prioritize them for appropriate retention strategies.

It uses churn probabilities generated by the trained LightGBM model to categorize customers into risk groups and create a customer-level retention priority list.

This notebook performs risk-based retention prioritization. It does not estimate causal uplift because the dataset does not contain actual retention campaign assignments or campaign outcomes.

## Key Activities

### 1. Load Customer Data

- Load the prepared customer feature table or available customer dataset.
- Inspect customer records and relevant features.
- Identify customer identifiers, churn information, monthly charges, tenure, and contract information.

### 2. Load the Trained LightGBM Model

- Load the saved model pipeline from the models directory.
- Prepare input features required by the trained model.
- Generate predicted churn probabilities for customers.

### 3. Categorize Churn Risk

Customers are grouped using the predicted churn probability:

- **Low Risk:** Predicted probability below 30%.
- **Medium Risk:** Predicted probability from 30% to below 60%.
- **High Risk:** Predicted probability of 60% or higher.

These thresholds are configurable business rules, not universal definitions of churn risk.

### 4. Develop Retention Priorities

- Assign priority categories based on estimated churn risk.
- Suggest appropriate retention actions for different risk levels.
- Identify customers who may need proactive engagement.
- Consider customer tenure, contract type, and monthly charges where available.

### 5. Use Supporting Analysis

Where the relevant outputs are available, the notebook can use:

- SHAP feature importance to understand which features influence model predictions.
- Kaplan–Meier survival estimates to examine cohort-level retention patterns.

The overall survival curve should not be interpreted as an individual customer's exact remaining lifetime.

### 6. Generate Visualizations and Reports

- Visualize the distribution of predicted churn risk.
- Summarize customer counts across risk groups.
- Examine observed churn patterns by risk group where actual churn labels are available.

## Business Value

This notebook helps a telecom business:

- Identify customers with a high predicted probability of churn.
- Prioritize customer-service and retention outreach.
- Understand how churn risk is distributed across customers.
- Prepare customer-level information for subsequent CLV and ROI analysis.

## Limitations

- Churn probabilities are model predictions, not certainties.
- Risk thresholds are configurable and should be evaluated against business needs.
- A high-risk customer is not necessarily more persuadable by a retention offer.
- SHAP explains model predictions but does not establish causality.
- Genuine uplift modeling requires treatment/control information and observed campaign outcomes.
